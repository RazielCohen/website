Active Shooter Response

Scenario-driven training on recognizing, responding to, and surviving an active threat — for armed and unarmed civilians alike. Covers movement, cover, communication, and decision-making under stress.

What it covers & what to bring

● What It Covers

  • Fundamentals Recap
  • Medical Intro
  • Advanced Malfunction Clearing
  • Advanced Barricade Usage
  • Tourniquet Application
  • Hostage Drills
  • Shooting Positions
  • Team Threat Drills
  • Self Aid
  • Single Hand Shooting

● What You Need

  • 1 Pistol
  • 1 Holster
  • 3 Magazines and carriers
  • 250 Rounds of ammunition
  • Eye and ear protection
  • Sun Hat
  • Long Pants
  • Closed-toe shoes
  • Food & water
⚠ This course involves rolling in mud and simulated blood as part of the scenario work — don't wear anything you care too much about!
While this training course is scheduled for up to 4 hours, the actual duration may vary depending on class size and participation. Smaller groups often allow us to move more efficiently through the material. Please note that the full value and curriculum are delivered regardless of course length.
