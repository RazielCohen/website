## Risks / Proposal-Impacting Concerns

- **R-001 (P0)** — The Premium tier's monthly raffle may implicate sweepstakes/lottery law. Recommend legal/compliance review.
- **R-002 (P1, partially mitigated)** — Hosting firearms/medical training video content may carry liability or age-verification considerations. The universal liability waiver (S-016) partially addresses this, but its enforceability for this content still needs legal confirmation.
- **R-003 (P1, partially mitigated)** — Qualification records likely carry record-retention, access-control, and chain-of-custody requirements. The client's explicit "records-keeping only, no liability" business model and required waiver (S-016) partially address this, but legal confirmation is still needed, along with the actual retention/access rules.
